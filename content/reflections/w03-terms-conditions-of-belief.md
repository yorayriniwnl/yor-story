Nobody asked you. That is the part that gets buried under every subsequent conversation about faith. We talk about belief, about doubt, about God and ritual and meaning — and all of it begins with you as a conscious participant making a deliberate choice. That conversation is a forgery. It starts too late. By the time anyone asks what you believe, the belief is already running.

The ceremony happened before language. The name was written in a register before you understood what registration meant. The prayer was in your ears before you knew what prayer was. You were enrolled.

> In contract law, there is a doctrine called incapacity. A minor cannot be legally bound to an agreement. The debt does not transfer. The obligation does not hold — because consent requires comprehension, and comprehension requires a mind developed enough to understand what it is agreeing to.

Religion found the workaround.

The workaround is this: install the belief before comprehension develops, and it will not feel like a belief. It will feel like reality. You do not question reality. Reality does not have an exit clause. Reality does not ask if you want to renew.

By seven, you know the words but not why. By fourteen, you know why — but the why was given to you. By twenty-five, you are explaining it to someone else. The installation is complete. The installer has left the building.

---

This is not about whether God exists. That is a different question, and honestly a more interesting one. This is about who signed on your behalf — and whether that signature holds under examination.

The most sophisticated part of the product is that the installation happens before the user knows there is a product. After that, the product reviews itself. The devotee is not a customer. The devotee is the proof of purchase.
